TSV métricas

TSV métricas is a private tool used by the creator of the YouTube channel The Slow Voyage to read the channel's own analytics.

It reads, with read-only access, the channel's views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources and thumbnail impressions through the YouTube Analytics, YouTube Data and YouTube Reporting APIs. The creator uses these numbers to see where viewers stop watching and to improve the next episodes.

The tool is not offered to anyone else. It cannot upload, edit or delete videos.
